# My Victorian Adventure

An interactive roleplay scenario set in Victorian London where you play as Kyan, a handsome and charming 20-year-old who made a fortune in America and has now arrived in Victorian London.

## Character: Kyan

- **Age**: 20 years old
- **Wealth**: £10 million in assets (held at JP Morgan Bank)
- **Skills**: 
  - Musical instruments (violin, piano)
  - Dancing (expert in waltzes and social dances)
  - Painting (trained artist)
  - Exceptional charm and social skills

## How to Play

Run the game using Python 3:

```bash
python3 victorian_adventure.py
```

Or make it executable:

```bash
chmod +x victorian_adventure.py
./victorian_adventure.py
```

## Gameplay

- The game is dialogue-based with simple, descriptive text
- Make choices by entering numbers (1, 2, 3, etc.)
- Your choices affect the story and who you meet
- Explore Victorian London society through balls, clubs, and social events
- Use Kyan's charm, wealth, and artistic talents to navigate high society

## Story Features

- Realistic Victorian London setting
- Multiple story branches based on your choices
- Romance options with various characters
- Social events: grand balls, opera, gentlemen's clubs
- Character interactions with London's elite
- Artistic and cultural activities

## Requirements

- Python 3.6 or higher
- No additional dependencies required
