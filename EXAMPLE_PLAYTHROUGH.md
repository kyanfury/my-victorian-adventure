# Example Playthrough - Victorian London Adventure

## Opening Scene

```
============================================================
         WELCOME TO VICTORIAN LONDON ADVENTURE
============================================================

You are Kyan - a handsome, charming 20-year-old who made
a fortune of £10 million in America. You have just arrived
in Victorian London with artistic talents in music, dance,
and painting. Your adventure begins now...
```

## Scene: Arrival at London Docks

The ship docks at the Thames. Fog rolls across Victorian London.
A dock worker greets you and offers to fetch a carriage.

**Your choices:**
1. Go to the finest hotel in Mayfair
2. Walk and explore the city
3. Visit JP Morgan bank

## Example Path: The Grand Ball

### Choice 1: The Langham Hotel
You arrive at London's most prestigious hotel in Mayfair.

### Choice 2: The Royal Suite
You settle into magnificent accommodations with high ceilings and fine furniture.

### Choice 3: Lady Catherine's Ball
You attend a grand ball where London's elite gather.

### Meeting Eleanor and Charlotte
Two beautiful ladies are introduced to you:
- **Eleanor Fitzroy**: Intelligent with green eyes
- **Charlotte Beaumont**: Blonde with a playful smile

### The Dance
You dance with Charlotte, showcasing your exceptional skills.
Your artistic talents and charm make a strong impression.

### Private Moment
On the moonlit terrace, Charlotte confesses she finds you
exciting and different from typical London gentlemen.
